Academic IoT prototype · 2024

Nature and Facilities Protector

Sensor acquisition → compact payload → LoRaWAN transport → decoding, storage, and alerts

Sensor coverage

Environmental, gas/smoke, location, light, motion, and power measurements were explored with modular WisBlock hardware.

Network boundary

Helium supplies LoRaWAN connectivity and incentivized coverage. The repository does not claim that raw sensor records are written to a blockchain.

Prototype limitation

This is an academic monitoring prototype, not a certified fire alarm or life-safety system.
